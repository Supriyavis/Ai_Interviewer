# 🚀 PrepWise AI – AI Interview Preparation Platform

PrepWise AI is an AI-powered interview preparation platform designed to help students and job seekers practice interviews in a smart and structured way. It simulates real interview environments and helps users improve their technical knowledge, communication skills, and confidence.

## Features

-  AI-based interview simulation  
-  Interactive and user-friendly interface  
-  Performance feedback and improvement tracking  
-  Technical, HR, and behavioral question practice  
-  Modern gradient UI (pink & navy blue theme)

##  Purpose

The main objective of PrepWise AI is to:
- Help users practice interviews anytime  
- Reduce interview fear and anxiety  
- Improve problem-solving and communication skills  
- Provide a real-world interview experience using AI
